# Sales Report — Q2 FY26 (Apr–Jun 2026)

Owner: Data & Analytics (#team-data). All figures are GMV (gross merchandise
value, before refunds) unless stated. Money shown in ₹ for readability; the
warehouse stores everything in integer paise/cents.

## Promotions

- **SUMMER20** (20% off) ran the first weekend of June: +24% orders that weekend,
  but AOV dipped ~9% for the period — expected for a percentage-off coupon.
- **WELCOME10** (new customers) drove first-order conversion up 1.1 pt.

## Watch-outs

- Cart abandonment rose to 66%, concentrated at the **payment step** — the Purchase
  team is investigating (see #team-purchase).
- Books had zero stock on the top title (`BOOK-RAG-01`) for 9 days; estimated
  ₹6–8 lakh of lost GMV. Inventory alerting is the follow-up.
